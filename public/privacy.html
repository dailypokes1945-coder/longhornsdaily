PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated November 17, 2026

Longhorns Daily is a headline site. You don't need an account to read it, there are no sign-up forms on the site, and we don't sell your personal information. This page explains what information is involved when you visit.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Basic technical data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit, to deliver the site, keep it secure and block abuse. We may see this only as aggregate traffic statistics (for example, the number of visits per day). See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies. Longhorns Daily does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Third-party services described below may set their own cookies when you use them.

Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and handle your request.

LINKS AND THIRD-PARTY CONTENT

Links to other sites. Most headlines link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for how they handle your information.

Embedded posts and videos. Some stories include posts or videos embedded from X, YouTube or Facebook. When those load, those services may receive information about your visit and set cookies under their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links. Some ticket and shop links, such as StubHub, are affiliate links, marked with an asterisk (*) and disclosed on the front page. When you click one, the retailer or its affiliate network (such as Partnerize) may use cookies to record that you came from Longhorns Daily so we can earn a commission. This doesn't change your price.

Newsletter, podcast and sponsors. If we link to a newsletter, podcast or sponsor, those run on outside platforms with their own privacy policies.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the limited information above to run and secure the site, understand overall traffic, reply to messages and earn affiliate commissions. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we share it only with the service providers listed above or when required by law.

YOUR CHOICES

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and still read the site. Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states), you may have rights to know about, correct or delete personal information about you. To make a request, email ads@longhornsdaily.com.

CHILDREN

Longhorns Daily is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we'll post the update here and change the date at the top.

CONTACT

Questions about privacy: ads@longhornsdaily.com.

Every headline links to its original source.
