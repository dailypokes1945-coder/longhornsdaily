ABOUT LONGHORNS DAILY

Longhorns Daily is a one-page headline site for Texas Longhorns athletics: football, men's and women's basketball, volleyball, baseball, softball, track and field, swimming and the rest of Texas sports. The front page is updated several times a day with the news that matters, plus a ticker of upcoming games.

We don't republish articles. Every headline is written in our own words and links straight to the original story from the outlet that reported it, including texaslonghorns.com, Inside Texas, Horns247, the Austin American-Statesman, Texas Longhorns On SI, Burnt Orange Nation, Orangebloods, KXAN, The Daily Texan, ESPN and others. Every day's front page is also saved in the archive.

INDEPENDENT

Longhorns Daily is an independent fan site run by a Longhorns fan. HOW THE SITE IS PAID FOR

Longhorns Daily is free to read. Some ticket and shop links are affiliate links: if you buy something after clicking one, Longhorns Daily may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Those links are marked with an asterisk (*). Sponsored placements are clearly labeled. Advertisers and affiliate partners have no say in which headlines run.
